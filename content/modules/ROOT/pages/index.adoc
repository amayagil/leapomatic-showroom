= Leapp-O-Matic: Automated RHEL 10 Upgrades with AAP
:navtitle: Welcome

Welcome to the *Leapp-O-Matic* demo. This is a guided *"Know & Show"* experience: it gives you, the presenter, clear talking points and a step-by-step flow so you can confidently show an associate, customer, or partner how Ansible Automation Platform (AAP) drives safe, automated in-place upgrades from RHEL 9 to RHEL 10.

The story you are telling is simple and powerful: *major OS upgrades are risky, and Leapp-O-Matic makes them repeatable, validated, and safe.* You will demonstrate how the `role-rhel10-upgrader` role — built on the LEAPP upgrade utility — treats every upgrade as a series of validated phases, stops unsafe upgrades *before* any destructive change, and upgrades compatible systems cleanly end to end.

[NOTE]
====
*Presenter note:* This is a demo, not a hands-on lab. Your goal is to *show and narrate*, not to have the audience perform the steps. The "What I say" blocks give you a ready-made talk track, and the "What I do" blocks tell you exactly which clicks to perform. Adapt the language to your audience, but keep the narrative thread intact: risk → validation → safe outcome.
====

== What This Demo Shows

* How the `role-rhel10-upgrader` role automates a RHEL 9 → RHEL 10 upgrade using LEAPP.
* How RHSM credentials are managed securely in AAP — never hardcoded.
* How a single AAP workflow orchestrates the upgrade across a mixed fleet of nodes.
* Why a workflow targeting under-resourced or incompatible nodes *fails safely* at preflight.
* Why a workflow targeting compatible nodes upgrades successfully — including automatic RHEL 9.x version normalization.
* How the upgrade is verified and how post-upgrade tasks such as configuration conflicts are handled.

== Who This Demo Is For

* Infrastructure and platform teams responsible for large RHEL fleets.
* Customers weighing the effort and risk of moving to RHEL 10.
* Partners and associates who want to understand the value of automating OS lifecycle with AAP.

The audience does *not* need deep Ansible expertise. Keep the message business-first: *predictable, low-risk upgrades at scale.*

== What You Will Need Before Presenting

* Access to the AAP Controller and the demo environment provided for this session.
* A valid Red Hat account with subscription entitlements (used to register systems during the demo).
* Familiarity with the flow below — do a dry run once before presenting live.
